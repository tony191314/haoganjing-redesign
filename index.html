What is Intravenous Laser

什麼是「靜脈雷射」？ Understanding the Technology

醫療用雷射中大抵可分兩大類「傳統雷射」及「低能量雷射」兩種。 傳統雷射又稱為熱雷射，大多利用其高熱能特性以達到止血或切割組織作用，多使用於醫美及眼科手術上。

而好甘淨靜脈雷射是低能量雷射稱為冷雷射，透過光吸收效應為血液行光合作用， 其電磁效應影響體內生理及代謝反應，主要以改善各式慢性疾病，成為預防及治療醫學的新趨勢， 被視為新世代延緩老化、活化循環功能以及增加免疫力的最佳利器。

"Medical lasers can be divided into two main categories: conventional lasers and low-energy lasers. Conventional lasers, also known as hot lasers, use their high heat energy to stop bleeding or cut tissue, and are mainly used for cosmetic and ophthalmic procedures."

While Haoganjing Intravenous Laser is a low-energy laser, also known as cold laser, which photosynthesizes the blood through light absorption, and its electromagnetic effect affects the physiological metabolism of the human body, and is mainly used to improve a variety of chronic diseases.
